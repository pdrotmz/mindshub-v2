# Domain Refinement

## Academic

### Course

A Course represents a learning program that students can enroll in.

### Lesson

A Lesson contains the homework of a course module.

### Business Rules

- Lessons have a deadline.
- Teachers define the number of questions (1–10).
- The number of attempts depends on the selected difficulty.
- Difficulty also influences the reward multiplier.

---

## Assessment

### Supply

A Supply contains the educational content of a course module.

### Business Rules

- Only teachers can submit a supply.
- The file format must be PDF.

---

## Gamification

### Business Rules

- Students earn points according to their performance.
- XP is awarded based on completed lessons
- Badges are unlocked after achieving specific milestones.
- Levels are determined by accumulated XP.

---

## Marketplace

### Business Rules

- Rewards can be redeemed using points.
- Redemptions cannot be canceled.
- Rewards have limited stock.
